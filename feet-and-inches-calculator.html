How to Convert Feet and Inches to Centimeters

Converting height or length measurements from the Imperial system (feet and inches) to the Metric system (centimeters and meters) is straightforward when you know the standard conversion factors:

Fact 1

1 Foot = 12 Inches = 30.48 cm

Fact 2

1 Inch = 2.54 cm Exact

The Standard Formula:

Total Centimeters = (Feet × 30.48) + (Inches × 2.54)

Step-by-Step Example Calculation:

Let's convert 5 feet 9 inches to centimeters:

  1. Convert feet to cm: 5 ft × 30.48 = 152.4 cm
  2. Convert inches to cm: 9 in × 2.54 = 22.86 cm
  3. Add the two values together: 152.4 + 22.86 = 175.26 cm
