How Does Exact Age Calculation Work?

Age calculation might seem simple at first glance, but calendar irregularities like varying month lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) and leap years require exact chronological date math:

  • Year Difference: We subtract birth year from target year, adjusting backward if your birthday has not yet occurred this year.
  • Month Difference: Calculated accurately by borrowing the correct number of days from the preceding month if current day is less than birth day.
  • Leap Years: Our algorithm includes all February 29th days passed over your lifetime.
  • Time units: Total days are multiplied by 24 hours and 1,440 minutes for precise life totals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the age calculator handle leap years? ▼

Our calculator accounts for all Gregorian leap years, ensuring accurate total day counts and exact month/day differences.

Can I calculate my age on a specific future or past date? ▼

Yes! You can adjust the "Age as of Date" field to calculate how old you will be on an upcoming retirement date or anniversary.

Is my birthdate recorded or stored? ▼

No. All date arithmetic occurs entirely inside your browser using client-side JavaScript. No dates are logged or stored.
