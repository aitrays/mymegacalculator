How to Calculate Room Paint Quantity

To estimate how much paint you will need for a room, follow this simple formula:

  1. Wall Perimeter: Add up the length of all four walls: 2 × (Length + Width).
  2. Gross Wall Area: Multiply the perimeter by the ceiling height.
  3. Deduct Openings: Subtract doors (~21 sq ft / 2 m² each) and windows (~15 sq ft / 1.4 m² each).
  4. Coats & Coverage: Multiply net area by the number of coats (usually 2), then divide by paint coverage per gallon (~350 sq ft/gal) or liter (~10 m²/L).
  5. Round Up: Always round up to the nearest whole can so you don't run out during finishing touches.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much wall area does 1 gallon of paint cover? ▼

One standard gallon of interior paint covers about 350 to 400 square feet with one coat. One liter covers roughly 10 square meters.

Why are 2 coats of paint recommended? ▼

Two coats provide true color opacity, hide underlying patches or drywall seams, and create a durable, washable paint finish that lasts for years.

Is my room data saved? ▼
