About MyMegaCalculator

Why Choose MyMegaCalculator?

MyMegaCalculator was built with one goal in mind: to provide simple, reliable, and private calculation tools that anyone can use without hassle. Whether you are planning a home renovation, checking your BMI and fitness goals, calculating loan payments, or solving everyday math, our calculators deliver accurate results in seconds.

Unlike many online tools, we never ask for your email, phone number, or any personal information. All calculations happen directly inside your web browser using JavaScript. Your inputs never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Hand-Coded for Accuracy

Every calculator on this site is hand-coded by our founder, Alex Morgan, a full-stack software engineer with over 8 years of professional experience. Each tool is thoroughly tested against known mathematical standards before going live. We regularly review and update our calculators to maintain accuracy and improve user experience.

Mobile-Friendly & Accessible

All our calculators are fully responsive and optimized for mobile phones, tablets, and desktops. You can use them on the job site, in the kitchen, or at your desk — wherever you need quick, reliable math. Our site is also built following WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards, making it usable for everyone.
