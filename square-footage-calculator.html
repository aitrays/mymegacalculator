How to Calculate Square Footage

Square footage measures the surface area in square feet. It is widely used in real estate, flooring, painting, and construction projects.

Area (Square Feet) = Length (feet) × Width (feet)

If your measurements include inches, convert the inches to a decimal fraction of a foot by dividing the number of inches by 12 (for example, 6 inches = 0.5 feet).

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert square feet to square meters?

Multiply your square footage by 0.092903 to get the total area in square meters.

What if my room is not a perfect rectangle?

Divide the irregular space into separate rectangular zones, calculate the square footage of each zone, and add them together.
