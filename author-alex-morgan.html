Alex Morgan is a full-stack software engineer and the founder of MyMegaCalculator. With over 8 years of professional experience building web applications for fintech and ed-tech companies, Alex combines technical precision with a passion for educational technology.

After repeatedly finding himself searching for simple, reliable calculators — whether for a home renovation project, a cooking recipe conversion, or a quick percentage check — Alex realized that most existing tools were either buried inside bloated mobile apps, required intrusive sign-ups, or displayed inaccurate results. That frustration became the foundation for MyMegaCalculator.

Every calculator on this site is hand-coded, thoroughly tested against known mathematical standards, and optimized for performance. Alex personally reviews each tool's accuracy, user interface, and mobile responsiveness before it goes live.

Education & Background

  • Former Senior Frontend Engineer at a leading ed-tech platform
  • Certified in Web Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

What Alex Writes About

On the MyMegaCalculator blog, Alex covers topics ranging from practical home improvement math and unit conversion guides to deeper dives into how calculators work under the hood. The goal is always the same: make complex math feel simple and intuitive.
