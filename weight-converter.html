Common Weight Conversion Formulas

Weight conversion involves translating values between the metric system (grams, kilograms) and the imperial system (pounds, ounces, stones). Here are the primary conversion factors used:

  • 1 Kilogram (kg) = 2.20462 Pounds (lbs)
  • 1 Pound (lbs) = 453.592 Grams (g)
  • 1 Stone (st) = 14 Pounds (lbs)
  • 1 Ounce (oz) = 28.3495 Grams (g)
