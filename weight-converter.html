Common Weight Conversion Formulas

Weight conversion translates values between the metric system (grams, kilograms) and the imperial system (pounds, ounces, stones).

  • 1 Kilogram (kg) = 2.20462 Pounds (lbs)
  • 1 Pound (lbs) = 453.592 Grams (g)
  • 1 Stone (st) = 14 Pounds (lbs)
  • 1 Ounce (oz) = 28.3495 Grams (g)

Choosing precision

Report only as many decimals as the scale, package, or specification supports. A conversion may produce many digits, but those digits do not make an imprecise measurement more accurate. Keep the original unit visible, check whether the unit is mass or volume, and document any required upward rounding for postage or compliance. For repeated work, use the same scale and procedure each time.

Weight Conversion FAQs

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

One kilogram equals approximately 2.20462 pounds. For everyday estimates, 1 kg is often rounded to 2.2 lb.

How many grams are in an ounce?

One avoirdupois ounce equals approximately 28.3495 grams. This is the standard ounce used for most food, household, and shipping measurements.
