Our Story

About MyMegaCalculator

We are a small team of engineers, designers, and math enthusiasts building free tools that solve real everyday problems.

Who We Are

MyMegaCalculator was founded in 2025 with a simple mission: to make complex calculations accessible to everyone. Whether you are renovating your home, managing personal finances, converting units for a recipe, or simply curious about measurements, our tools are designed to give you accurate answers in seconds — with zero learning curve.

Our team combines expertise in software engineering, user experience design, and applied mathematics. We believe that great tools do not need to be complicated. Every calculator on this site is built with precision, tested thoroughly, and optimized to work flawlessly on both desktop and mobile devices.

What We Do

We specialize in creating high-precision online calculators across multiple categories:

  • Home Improvement: Tile calculators, square footage estimators, and flooring tools that help contractors and DIY homeowners plan projects accurately.
  • Unit Conversions: Weight, length, and height converters that bridge imperial and metric systems for international users.
  • Financial Math: Percentage calculators for discounts, increases, margins, and tax calculations.
  • Educational Content: Step-by-step guides, formula explanations, and reference tables that teach you the "why" behind every calculation.

Why We Built This

The idea for MyMegaCalculator came from a frustrating experience: trying to find a simple, accurate tile calculator that did not require downloading an app or creating an account. Every tool we found was either cluttered with ads, required sign-ups, or gave incorrect results.

We decided to build something better. A calculator site that respects your time, your privacy, and your intelligence. No pop-ups. No data collection. Just fast, accurate tools and clear explanations.
