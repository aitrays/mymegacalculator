How to Calculate Tiles for Floors and Walls

Calculating the correct amount of tiles prevents shortages mid-project or overspending on excess materials. Here is how tile estimation works:

  1. Measure the length and width of the room and multiply them to get the total surface area.
  2. Calculate the area of a single tile by multiplying its length and width.
  3. Divide the room area by the individual tile area to find the base number of tiles.
  4. Add a waste percentage (usually 10% for straight layouts, 15% to 20% for diagonal cuts or rooms with pillars) to account for cuts and breakages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I add a waste percentage?

Cutting tiles to fit corners, edges, and pipes inevitably results in discarded scraps and occasional breakage. Adding 10% extra ensures you won't run short.

How do I measure irregular rooms?

Break down complex or L-shaped rooms into smaller rectangular sections, calculate the area of each section separately, and add them together.
