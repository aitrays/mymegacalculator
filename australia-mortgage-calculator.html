Another good habit is to check the direction of the result. If a higher tax rate produces a lower tax amount, something is wrong. If a higher interest rate produces a lower repayment on an otherwise identical loan, review the inputs. Sanity checks are simple, but they catch many data-entry mistakes. A result that looks precise can still be wrong if the input units are wrong, such as entering months where years are expected or entering a percentage as a decimal when the field expects a whole number.
